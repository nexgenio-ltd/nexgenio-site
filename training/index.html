Certification Body
What is PECB?

PECB (Professional Evaluation and Certification Board) is a global certification body providing training, examination, and certification across a wide range of standards — including information security (ISO/IEC 27001), business continuity (ISO 22301), AI management (ISO/IEC 42001), privacy (ISO/IEC 27701), and emerging frameworks like the EU’s NIS2 Directive and DORA. PECB certification demonstrates practical skill in implementing these frameworks, not just theoretical knowledge.
