What is ISO/IEC 27001?

ISO/IEC 27001 is the internationally recognized standard for information security management systems (ISMS) — a structured framework for identifying information security risks and putting the right controls in place to manage them. It has become the default reference point customers, regulators, and business partners expect when they ask an organization to prove it takes information security seriously, and it underpins compliance obligations across a wide range of other frameworks, including NIS2 and DORA.

Organizations building or maturing an ISMS need people who can do more than quote the standard’s clauses — they need someone who can actually implement it. This course builds that competency directly: you learn how to scope an ISMS, run risk assessments, select and implement controls, and prepare an organization for third-party certification audit — the same practical skills employers and certification bodies expect from an ISO/IEC 27001 implementer.

Which credential will I earn?

Everyone who attends this course sits the same exam — the PECB Certified ISO/IEC 27001 Lead Implementer exam. What differs is which credential you can apply for afterward, based on your prior professional experience. There’s no separate exam or extra study required for a higher tier — it’s the same certification, recognized at a level that matches your background.

Credential Experience required ISMS project experience
Provisional ImplementerNoneNone
Implementer2 years (1 in information security mgmt)200+ hours
Lead Implementer5 years (2 in information security mgmt)300+ hours
Senior Lead Implementer10 years (7 in information security mgmt)1,000+ hours

New to the field? You’ll qualify for Provisional Implementer immediately after passing — a fully valid PECB credential — and can apply for a higher tier later as your experience grows, without retaking the course.
