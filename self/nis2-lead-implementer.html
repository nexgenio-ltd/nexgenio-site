What is the NIS2 Directive?

The NIS2 Directive (EU) 2022/2555 strengthens cybersecurity requirements across critical infrastructure and essential service providers in the European Union. It broadens the scope of covered sectors, tightens security obligations, and imposes stricter incident-reporting requirements and penalties than its predecessor — non-compliance carries real regulatory and financial consequences, not just a compliance checkbox.

Organizations facing this shift need people who can actually lead the response, not just interpret the text. This course builds that competency directly: you gain a working understanding of the directive’s requirements, practical implementation strategies, and the skills to assess risk, manage incidents, and communicate cybersecurity decisions to stakeholders at every level of an organization — the same competencies regulators and employers expect from someone responsible for NIS2 compliance.

Which credential will I earn?

Everyone who attends this course sits the same exam — the PECB Certified NIS2 Directive Lead Implementer exam. What differs is which credential you can apply for afterward, based on your prior professional experience. There’s no separate exam or extra study required for a higher tier — it’s the same certification, recognized at a level that matches your background.

Credential Experience required Cybersecurity management experience
Provisional ImplementerNoneNone
Implementer2 years (1 in cybersecurity)200+ hours of project activity
Lead Implementer5 years (2 in cybersecurity)300+ hours of project activity
Senior Lead Implementer10 years (7 in cybersecurity)1,000+ hours of project activity

New to the field? You’ll qualify for Provisional Implementer immediately after passing — a fully valid PECB credential — and can apply for a higher tier later as your experience grows, without retaking the course.
