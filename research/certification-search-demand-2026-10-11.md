# Search demand and competition — 11 certification training terms (Europe/UK, English)

Researched via Perplexity (sonar-pro), 2026-10-11. Multiple rephrased queries per term, across
UK and Europe geography, against Ahrefs, Semrush, and Google Keyword Planner framing.

## Headline finding — read this before the table

**No numeric monthly search-volume figure, and no measured 12-month trend direction, could be
sourced for ANY of the 11 terms.** This was tested repeatedly and consistently across multiple
query phrasings, tool mentions (Ahrefs, Semrush, Google Keyword Planner, Google Trends), and
geography framings (UK alone, Europe aggregate). Perplexity has no live connector into Ahrefs
Keywords Explorer, Semrush Keyword Overview, Google Keyword Planner, or Google Trends — it can
only surface what a prior web page has published, and no training provider, SEO agency, or case
study publicly discloses keyword-volume numbers for these specific PECB/ISO certification
phrases. This is a **data-access gap in the tool used**, not a verified finding that volume is
zero. A defensible numeric answer requires running these phrases directly in Ahrefs Keywords
Explorer, Semrush, or an authenticated Google Keyword Planner account, split UK vs. an explicit
list of European countries (Google Trends/Planner do not treat "Europe" as one native market).

Everything in the table below that is not a directly cited figure is explicitly flagged as
**inference from indirect market signals** (number of active competing training providers found
in search results, job-vacancy data where available, regulatory-deadline timing) — never
presented as a measured search number.

## Direct answer to the format question

**Self-paced vs. live instructor-led: demand does not clearly fragment into two separate
queries, based on the evidence available.** No source found any case where "[certification]
self paced" or "[certification] online course" was shown to carry meaningful independent search
volume separate from the plain "[certification] training" / "[certification] course" query. The
one partial counter-signal is on the **supply side, not the demand side**: a handful of
providers (GRC Solutions for ISO 27001 and for DORA Foundation; Abilene Academy for IEC 62443)
have built a dedicated self-paced/self-study product page in addition to their general
certification page — meaning at least these providers believe the self-paced buyer is a distinct
enough segment to warrant a separate page. But even these providers keep ONE generic
certification page as the primary/default page and treat the format-specific page as a secondary,
supplementary product listing — not as the primary SEO landing page.

**Conclusion on landing-page strategy:** the evidence supports **one generic landing page per
certification** (e.g. "ISO 27001 Lead Implementer Training") carrying all qualifiers — training,
course, certification, exam, online — as on-page content/sections, with format (self-paced vs.
live) handled as a *selector or secondary section on that one page*, not as a separate page
competing for its own keyword. Building two full separate landing pages per certification purely
to split self-paced vs. live is not supported by any volume evidence found; it would only be
justified if a live Ahrefs/Semrush/Keyword-Planner pull later shows independent, non-trivial
volume for the format-qualified query — which this research could not confirm or rule out due to
tool-access limits.

## Honest limitations of this research

1. Perplexity has no live connector to Ahrefs, Semrush, Google Keyword Planner, or Google Trends
   — it can only find figures that a third party has already published online, and none have for
   these specific phrases. This is a **tool-access ceiling**, not proof that volume is zero or
   negligible.
2. Everything presented as "dominant qualifier" or relative-demand ranking in this report is
   **inference from provider count and page naming conventions**, explicitly flagged as such —
   never a measured figure.
3. The one genuinely measured, citable figure found in this entire research — UK ISO 27001 Lead
   Implementer job vacancies rising from 24 to 61 year-on-year (itjobswatch.co.uk) — is a
   **hiring-demand** signal, not a search-demand signal. It is included because it is real and
   sourced, but it should not be conflated with search volume.
4. To get a defensible numeric answer to the original question, the next step is a direct pull
   from Ahrefs Keywords Explorer or Semrush Keyword Overview (UK + named EU countries, English,
   trailing-12-month history) for all 11 terms plus their training/course/certification/exam/
   online/self-paced variants — this requires tool access this research session did not have.
