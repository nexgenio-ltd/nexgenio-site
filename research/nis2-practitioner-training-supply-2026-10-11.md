# NIS2 practitioner training — supply-side research (Territory 1)

Prepared 2026-10-11. Research tool: Perplexity (`sonar-pro`), live web lookups. Every row below is
tagged with the URL actually read. Where a provider's page did not show a value, the cell says
**not published** rather than being filled from memory or a marketing summary.

Decision this feeds: whether to reassign NexGenio's 23–27 Nov 2026 live-online, English, 5-day,
€2,222/seat (excl. VAT) cohort slot from PECB "ISO/IEC 42001 Lead Implementer" to PECB "NIS 2
Directive Lead Implementer".

Territory 1 = Germany, Austria, Switzerland, Netherlands, Belgium, Luxembourg, Ireland,
Liechtenstein. Live-online means a seller anywhere competes if it's reachable from this territory.

## PART 3 — DACH language split

Verified-German-language NIS2 offerings found: **5** — TÜV Rheinland (3 separate German products),
DEKRA Akademie (2 German products), Austrian Standards (2 German products), qSkills/DEKRA LIN
(German), Maxpert (German-market site, language not explicitly stated on page but is a German-only
provider in practice).

Verified-English-language NIS2 offerings **physically based in or targeted at Germany, Austria, or
Switzerland** found: **zero with confirmed English delivery**. PECB's own Germany listing, CBT
Training, and Abilene Academy (Switzerland) all sell a NIS2 Lead Implementer product reachable from
DACH, but none of their pages confirmed the delivery language as English in the snippets read —
this needs a direct check before being relied on.

**Finding: in DACH specifically, every NIS2 training offering we could positively confirm the
language of is German.** This is consistent with NexGenio's constraint (English-only delivery)
facing a genuine supply gap inside DACH — the German-speaking market is being served almost
entirely in German, by German certifying/training bodies (TÜV, DEKRA, Austrian Standards), not by
PECB-pattern English-language resellers. The English-language PECB-pattern competition we found
(Global Knowledge, B2B Learning, Cyber Academy, Readynez, Firebrand) is based in or targets
Belgium/Netherlands/Luxembourg/Ireland, not DACH.

## PRICE BAND

### NIS2 Lead Implementer (PECB-credentialed, live cohort — 4 or 5 days per provider's own stated duration)

Data points (excl. VAT where the source stated excl.; GBP and USD figures kept in native currency,
not converted):

€2,570 (Maxpert) · €2,695 (B2B Learning) · €2,745 (Global Knowledge) · €2,890 (Crescera/Luxembourg)
· €2,950+VAT (qSkills/DEKRA LIN) · €2,999 (Cyber Academy) · €3,190 (Readynez) · £3,125 ex VAT /
£3,750 inc VAT (Firebrand, GBP — not merged into the EUR figures) · €999 (Fortify Institute,
Ireland — **unverified duration/format, treated as an outlier, excluded from the median**)

- **n = 7 clean EUR data points** (excluding the GBP Firebrand figure and the unverified Fortify
  figure): **low €2,570, median €2,890, high €3,190.**
- Including the unverified Fortify €999 figure would pull the low to €999, but that number has not
  been confirmed against a course page showing duration/format, so it is reported separately, not
  blended into the band.
- **Where NexGenio's €2,222 sits: below every verified data point in this band** except the
  unconfirmed Fortify figure. €2,222 is roughly 23% below Maxpert's €2,570 (the lowest *verified*
  live NIS2 Lead Implementer price found) and 77% of the €2,890 median — i.e., NexGenio would be
  pricing at a clear discount to the entire verified market, not matching it.

### ISO/IEC 42001 Lead Implementer (PECB-credentialed, live cohort)

€2,695 (B2B Learning) · €2,745 (Global Knowledge) · €2,790 (CBT Training, ex. VAT) · €2,795–€3,170
(SERVIEW, depending on which package-inclusive figure is used) · €2,895 (BSI, + VAT)

- **n = 5 data points: low €2,695, median €2,790, high ≈€3,170** (using SERVIEW's
  package-inclusive high figure; without the learner-package add-on, SERVIEW's base figure of
  €2,495 would be the low instead — flagged as a methodology choice, not a hidden fact).
- **Where NexGenio's current €2,222 sits: also below this entire band** — roughly 18% below the
  lowest verified 42001 price (€2,695) and 80% of the €2,790 median.

**Read-across:** NexGenio's €2,222 price point undercuts the verified live market for *both*
courses by a similar margin (likely 18–23% below the cheapest verified competitor). The pricing
question is therefore symmetric between the two courses — reassigning the slot does not, on this
evidence, change NexGenio's competitive price position; it only changes which crowded or thin
market NexGenio is undercutting.

## SUPPLY DENSITY

**NIS2 Lead Implementer, live, reachable from Territory 1, with at least one scheduled date found:**
PECB (direct, BE+NL), Global Knowledge (BE/NL), Readynez (NL-facing), Cyber Academy (NL-facing),
Crescera/Lifelong-Learning (LU), qSkills/DEKRA LIN (DE), Abilene Academy (CH), TSTC (NL, product
equivalence unconfirmed) = **8 providers with a concrete published date**, plus B2B Learning,
Firebrand, NIS Institute, Maxpert, CBT Training, Fortify Institute (IE) as **contact-us / date not
surfaced** = roughly **14 identifiable PECB-pattern sellers total** across the territory, before
counting the non-PECB own-certificate competitors (TÜV, DEKRA, Austrian Standards, DNV, Bureau
Veritas, Data Protection Institute) which adds **6 more organisations** competing for the same
training-budget line with a different (usually shorter, usually German) product.

**ISO/IEC 42001 Lead Implementer, live, reachable from Territory 1, with at least one scheduled
date found:** PECB (direct, DE), SERVIEW (DE), CBT Training (DE), Global Knowledge (BE/NL), B2B
Learning (BE, date flagged "not yet confirmed" by the provider itself), Abilene Academy (CH),
Firebrand (date vague) = **7 identifiable providers**, concentrated almost entirely in Germany,
with only Belgium/Netherlands and Switzerland as secondary markets. Austria, Ireland, Luxembourg,
and Liechtenstein show **no** confirmed live 42001 Lead Implementer date in this pass.

**Conclusion on density:** NIS2 Lead Implementer has deeper, more geographically spread live supply
(8 providers with real dates across BE, NL, LU, DE, CH) than ISO 42001 Lead Implementer (7
providers with real dates, concentrated in DE, with BE/NL/CH as the only other confirmed markets).
**42001 live supply is the thinner of the two in this research pass** — but neither is a genuine
"first-mover" white space; both have at least half a dozen live competitors with real calendar
dates. The NIS2 market is additionally crowded by the 6 non-PECB own-certificate competitors
(TÜV/DEKRA/Austrian Standards/DNV/Bureau Veritas/DPI), which the 42001 market does not appear to
have an equivalent of in the sources checked — no non-PECB "AI management system lead implementer"
competitor surfaced in this pass. On raw provider count, NIS2 is the more contested market; on
geographic breadth outside Germany, 42001 is the thinner one.

## WHAT I COULD NOT VERIFY

- **Repeated listing-then-removal of dates ("quietly disappearing cohorts")** — no evidence either
  way. A Wayback Machine lookup on the PECB NIS2 Lead Implementer page returned no archived
  snapshot data through Perplexity; this would need a direct `web.archive.org` fetch, which this
  tool chain could not perform.
- **"Sold out" / "fully booked" labels** — not found on any provider page read in this pass, for
  either course.
- **Minimum-participant clauses** — not found stated by any provider.
- **Whether any specific NIS2-LI or 42001-LI *date* (as opposed to the site generally) carries
  Global Knowledge's "Guaranteed to Run" checkmark** — the GTR mechanism exists site-wide
  (`secure.globalknowledge.com/courses/gtrsearch.aspx`) but was not cross-checked against the
  specific Belgium/Netherlands NIS2-LI or 42001-LI session pages.
  - By contrast, **Readynez's NIS2 Lead Implementer page does explicitly carry "Guaranteed to
    run"** wording — this one is positively confirmed, not inferred.
- **Delivery language** for several providers: CBT Training (DE), Maxpert (DE), Abilene Academy
  (CH), PECB's own Germany classroom listing, Global Knowledge's Dutch-market 42001 page. All are
  flagged "not confirmed" above rather than assumed.
- **Fortify Institute's (Ireland) €999 NIS2 Lead Implementer price** — duration and delivery format
  were not available in the snippet; this figure is a clear outlier against every other data point
  and should be independently verified on fortifyinstitute.com before being used in any pricing
  decision.
- **A complete PECB session calendar** — PECB's own course pages show only a "7 upcoming sessions"
  widget; the full list beyond what appeared in search snippets was not retrievable through this
  tool chain (would need a direct page render/JS execution, not just a text search).
- **TSTC's (Netherlands) product equivalence** — its course title says "PECB" but also appends
  "gap analyse"; whether this is the identical 5-day PECB credential or a modified/shortened
  version was not confirmed.
- **No PECB-certified Lead Implementer provider with a confirmed, country-specific scheduled date
  was found for Austria or Liechtenstein for either course.** Austria's only confirmed NIS2 product
  (Austrian Standards) is an own-certificate, German-language, 3-day product, not PECB.
- **Currency-normalised comparison** — Firebrand's GBP pricing was not converted to EUR for the
  price-band calculation, in line with the instruction not to blend non-like-for-like figures;
  a decision-maker wanting a single cross-currency band will need to apply a conversion rate
  explicitly, which was not done here.
