The deadline

Your 2027 submission window

The reference date is fixed for everyone. The window, the format and the portal are set by your own authority, and they are not the same.

  • Reference date for the register: 31 December 2026
  • Collection by national authorities in Q1 2027 — for example CySEC by 28 February, MFSA from 1 January to 22 March, ACPR by 31 March
  • Formats differ by authority: xBRL-CSV, plain CSV or the authority's own template
  • Some authorities require registration on their reporting portal beforehand

Dates as published by the authorities at the time of writing — check your authority's current notice.
