The obligation

A staff duty the management body answers for

NIS2 places the training requirement in two places, and they do different work. Article 21(2)(g) requires the entity to adopt “basic cyber hygiene practices and cybersecurity training” as one of its risk-management measures. That is a duty about the people who build, run and secure the systems.

Member States shall ensure that the management bodies of essential and important entities approve the cybersecurity risk-management measures taken by those entities in order to comply with Article 21, oversee its implementation and can be held liable for infringements by the entities of that Article. Article 20(1), Directive (EU) 2022/2555

Article 20(1) then puts approval of those measures, and oversight of their implementation, on the management body itself, with liability attached. The board does not attend the courses on this page. It is the body that has to be able to show its staff are trained to the tasks they hold.

Source: Articles 20 and 21 of Directive (EU) 2022/2555 on EUR-Lex. Your operative obligation sits in your own country's transposing law, in the Netherlands the Cyberbeveiligingswet.

Questions

Frequently asked

Does NIS2 require staff cybersecurity training?
Article 21(2)(g) of Directive (EU) 2022/2555 lists “basic cyber hygiene practices and cybersecurity training” among the cybersecurity risk-management measures that essential and important entities must take. It is written as a measure the entity adopts, so it is evidenced at the level of the function.
Is this the same as the board training duty?
No, and they are satisfied separately. Article 20(2) requires members of the management body themselves to follow training. Article 21(2)(g) is the measure that covers the staff who implement and run the security programme. Article 20(1) connects them: the management body approves the Article 21 measures and oversees their implementation, so it is the body that has to be able to show the staff training is in place. The board duty has its own page, NIS2 board training.
Why offer ISO/IEC 27001 alongside the NIS2 course?
The NIS2 security measures in Article 21 are implemented and evidenced through a management system, and ISO/IEC 27001 is the standard that defines one. The two overlap across risk assessment, access control, supplier security, incident handling and business continuity. The Article 21 to Annex A mapping sets out where.
