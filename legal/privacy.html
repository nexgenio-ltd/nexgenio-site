Covers nexgenio.com (apex site), nexgenio.com/self/ (course store), and nexgenio.com/blog/ (NexGenio Insights). Structured consistently with NexGenio’s existing OntoVault privacy policy. Prepared as a working draft for lawyer review before publication.

1. Who We Are

NexGenio LTD (“we”, “us”, “our”) is the data controller for personal data processed through nexgenio.com and its subpages. Registered in Malta, company number C 88842, VAT MT25941925, registered address 36 St Dminika Street, Victoria (Gozo) VCT9030, Malta. Contact: toby@nexgenio.com.

2. What Data We Collect

  • Course purchases (nexgenio.com/self/): name, email address, billing address, VAT identification number (if provided for reverse charge), and payment confirmation details from Stripe (we do not receive or store full card numbers).
  • Contact/enquiry forms (if present on the apex site): name, email address, and message content.
  • Blog (nexgenio.com/blog/): currently no newsletter/subscription data is collected — the native signup form has been disabled pending a proper CRM-integrated implementation.
  • Website analytics: see Draft Flag below.

3. How We Use Your Data (Purposes and Legal Basis)

  • To process and fulfil course purchases — legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To comply with tax and accounting obligations (VAT records, invoicing) — legal basis: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).
  • To respond to enquiries submitted via contact forms — legal basis: legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), or contract performance where the enquiry leads to a service agreement.

4. Payment Processing

Payments are processed by Stripe, Inc., acting as our payment processor. Stripe processes payment data under its own privacy policy and data processing agreement, available at stripe.com/privacy. Card details are entered directly into Stripe’s secure checkout and are not received or stored by NexGenio.

5. Sharing Data with PECB

When you purchase a course, your name and email address are manually entered by our Director into the official PECB partner platform (myPECB) in order to provision your access to course materials and register you for the relevant certification examination. This is a manual process performed by a NexGenio staff member, not an automated data transfer. PECB acts as an independent data controller for this purpose — see PECB’s own privacy policy for how they handle your data.

7. Your Rights Under GDPR

As a data subject, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request erasure of your data, subject to our legal retention obligations (e.g. tax records)
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Request data portability
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Information and Data Protection Commissioner (IDPC), Malta’s supervisory authority, or your own EU member state’s equivalent authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact toby@nexgenio.com.
