Each course listing on the Site describes: the certification covered (e.g. NIS2 Directive Lead Implementer, DORA Lead Manager, ISO/IEC 27001 Lead Implementer, and others), the format (Self-Study or eLearning), the issuing body (PECB), and the price. eLearning includes video instruction in addition to the written materials included in Self-Study; both formats include the official PECB examination voucher and certification eligibility upon passing.

4. Price and Payment

All prices displayed on the Site are shown exclusive of VAT. VAT is calculated at checkout based on the Customer’s location and, for business customers, whether a valid EU VAT identification number is provided.

  • Business customers (B2B) providing a valid EU VAT number: the reverse-charge mechanism applies under Article 44/196 of Council Directive 2006/112/EC. No VAT is charged by NexGenio; the Customer is responsible for accounting for VAT in their own jurisdiction.
  • Consumers and business customers without a valid VAT number (B2C): VAT is charged at the applicable rate for Malta, or the Customer’s own EU member state where required by the EU VAT e-commerce rules for electronically supplied services.

Payment is processed via Stripe, a third-party payment processor. NexGenio does not store full payment card details.

5. Order Process

By completing checkout and clicking the payment confirmation button, the Customer places an order that carries an obligation to pay. NexGenio will make clear, directly before the Customer completes payment, the course selected, the total price including applicable VAT, and confirmation that placing the order involves a binding obligation to pay.

6. Delivery and Access

Following successful payment, we will process your course access via the official PECB platform (myPECB). This involves a manual verification and provisioning step on our part before access is granted. We aim to complete this within 2 business days of payment. The Customer will receive instructions for accessing their course by email once access has been provisioned.

7. Right of Withdrawal

Consumers within the EU ordinarily have a right to withdraw from a distance contract within 14 days of the contract being concluded, without giving any reason, in accordance with Directive 2011/83/EU.

Exception for digital content: because the courses purchased on this Site are digital content not supplied on a tangible medium, and access is provided promptly upon payment, the right of withdrawal is lost once the Customer has given express consent to immediate performance and has acknowledged that they thereby lose their right of withdrawal. This consent and acknowledgment will be captured via an explicit checkbox at checkout, separate from and in addition to acceptance of these Terms.
